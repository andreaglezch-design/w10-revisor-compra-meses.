Installment Purchase Check

A check on your side, before you pay. It sells nothing and never sends you to a store.

What you must pay every month, not counting your installments. Add up what you pay each month on Kueski, Aplazo, Mercado Pago, stores, etc. If none, enter 0. If the app charges interest or fees, enter that monthly amount here. If left empty, I divide the price by the months.

This tool is informational and is not financial advice. The rules are a simple example and are shown below. Your data is not stored: it lives only on this page. No commissions, no affiliates, no checkout links. Funded by the person using it or an aligned benefit, never by stores or lenders.

How it decides (example rules)
  • Skip: if after your expenses, installments and this purchase you would have less than 0 left, or if your installments (including the new one) exceed 30% of your income.
  • Wait: if you would have less than 15% of your income left, if your installments exceed 20%, or if information is missing.
  • Buy: in any other case.

The percentages are an adjustable example. They do not replace an advisor.
